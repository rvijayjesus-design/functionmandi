About FunctionMandi

FunctionMandi is a local event vendor booking platform built for India — connecting customers with trusted caterers, decorators, photographers, and venues in their own city and language.

🎯 Our Mission

Make event planning simple, transparent, and accessible for every city and town — not just metros.

✅ Verified Vendors

Every vendor can be Aadhaar/GST verified, so customers book with confidence.

🗣️ Local Language First

Available in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, and English to reach every customer comfortably.

🔒 Secure Bookings

Advance payments are held securely and released to vendors only after the event is completed.
